## What you get

<div class="lu-section-head" markdown>

<div>

<h3>Made to feel like television</h3>

</div>

A guide you can browse while the current channel keeps playing, channels that stay on schedule, and controls that work from a keyboard or a TV remote.

</div>

<div class="lu-board" markdown>

<article class="lu-card lu-card-guide" markdown>

<span class="lu-kicker">Guide</span>

### Browse without missing a minute

The channel you're watching keeps playing in the corner while you look through the next two, three or four hours. In the Player, press ++arrow-up++ for a Mini Guide over the picture.

<figure class="lu-shot"><img src="images/placeholder-16x9.webp" alt="Placeholder for a Mini Guide over the Player screenshot." width="1600" height="900"></figure>

</article>

<article class="lu-card lu-card-builder" markdown>

<span class="lu-kicker">Channel Builder</span>

### Channels from what you already have

Generate a whole lineup at once, or hand-pick a custom channel in Channel Studio.

<div class="lu-chips" aria-label="Channel sources"><span>Collections</span><span>Playlists</span><span>Genres</span><span>Decades</span><span>Studios</span><span>Actors</span><span>Directors</span><span>Recently added</span></div>

</article>

<article class="lu-card" markdown>

<span class="lu-kicker">Playback</span>

### Plays your files as they are

Files play directly from your Plex server. On Windows, fullscreen can switch your display to HDR to match the video.

<figure class="lu-shot"><img src="images/placeholder-16x9.webp" alt="Placeholder for an on-screen playback controls screenshot." width="1600" height="900"></figure>

</article>

<article class="lu-card" markdown>

<span class="lu-kicker">Controls</span>

### Keyboard or remote

On a keyboard: number keys change channel, ++a++ and ++c++ open audio and subtitle tracks, and ++s++ sets a sleep timer. On a TV remote: the D-pad, Channel Up and Down, and the Player controls.

</article>

<article class="lu-card" markdown>

<span class="lu-kicker">Comfort</span>

### Your way to watch

Four themes, reduced motion, larger focus indicators, subtitle styling, and optional DVR-style pause and seek.

</article>

## Questions

<div class="lu-section-head" markdown>

<div>

<h3>Before you install</h3>

</div>

</div>

<div class="lu-faq" markdown>

<div>

<details open><summary>What do I need?</summary>

A Plex account with access to a Plex Media Server that has at least one movie or TV library, and a Windows 10 or 11 PC, or an Android TV, Google TV or Fire TV device running Android 9 or later. A Mac version is in private testing.

</details>

<details><summary>Does switching channels restart the movie?</summary>

No. Each channel runs on a fixed schedule, so tuning in joins the program partway through, like live TV.

</details>

<details><summary>Which Fire TV devices work?</summary>

Fire TV devices on Android-based Fire OS that run Android 9 or later on ARM. Devices on Vega OS can't install APKs.

</details>

</div>

<div>

<details><summary>Will regenerating remove my custom channels?</summary>

No. Generating a lineup never touches custom channels, and you review every change before it's applied.

</details>

<details><summary>Where is my Plex sign-in stored?</summary>

In your system's secure credential storage. Support reports are redacted before you copy them. Review them before sharing.

</details>

<details><summary>Is there a public release yet?</summary>

Check GitHub Releases. If no build is listed there, no public build is available yet.

</details>

</div>
