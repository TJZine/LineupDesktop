# Audio, subtitles and sleep

Choose which audio and subtitles you hear and see, or set Lineup to stop after a while.
{ .lead }

## Audio and subtitle tracks

On a keyboard, press ++a++ for audio or ++c++ for subtitles. On TV, use the audio and subtitle actions in the Player controls. You can open the track lists directly from Now Playing when that track type is available. Unavailable actions stay disabled.

The panel first focuses your selected track or **Off** when subtitles are off. It marks a change pending until the player confirms it. If the change fails, the previous track remains selected and the panel shows an error. Closing the drawer returns focus to the action that opened it.

Languages use their own names. When tracks share a language but have different regions, the primary label adds the region. In other cases the region appears as a secondary detail. The on-screen controls use the same primary label.

See [Subtitle appearance](subtitle-appearance.md) to change how captions look.

## Sleep timer

Press ++s++ or open **Sleep timer** from the Player controls. Choose **Off**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, or **90 minutes**. Choosing a duration again starts a fresh countdown. The on-screen controls show the timer state.

The timer continues during pauses, channel or program changes, Guide browsing, and other overlays. Manually stopping playback, signing out, or closing Lineup cancels it. Closing the picker returns focus to the action that opened it.
