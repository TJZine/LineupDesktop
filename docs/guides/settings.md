# Settings

Open Settings to adjust how Lineup looks, plays, and connects to Plex.
{ .lead }

Settings save when you change them. The control you're editing is usually disabled while its change saves. If saving fails, Lineup keeps the previous setting and shows an error. [Subtitle appearance](subtitle-appearance.md#preview-and-saving) describes how those controls work while saving.

| Category | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | **Theme** offers **Ember & Steel**, **Slate & Pine**, **Mint Noir**, and **Satellite Blue**. **Player overlays** has three transparency choices. **Guide information background** offers **Artwork colors**, **Theme background**, and **Artwork backdrop**. **Use title artwork** shows Plex title artwork where available. |
| **Guide** | **Visible hours** offers 2, 3, or 4 hours. Use **Show channel sources** and **Show now playing in Guide** to choose which extra details the Guide shows. When rows are short, such as with larger text, the Guide leaves out the channel source to keep each row readable. |
| **Playback** | **Player controls auto-hide** offers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, or 15 seconds. See [The Player](player.md) for **DVR playback controls** and the Windows option **Match display HDR in fullscreen**, which is on by default. |
| **Subtitles** | See [Subtitle appearance](subtitle-appearance.md) for the preview, style controls, and device settings. |
| **Accessibility** | **Reduce motion** shortens or removes movement across management screens, Guide, and Player. **Large focus indicators** makes keyboard and remote focus easier to see. |
| **Account** | See [Profiles and servers](profiles-and-servers.md) to change profile or server, show the picker on startup, or sign out. |
| **Support** | Use **Record redacted diagnostics** and **Open Diagnostics** for [support reports](support-reports.md). On macOS and TV, **Open Licenses** opens third-party notices and source information. |
