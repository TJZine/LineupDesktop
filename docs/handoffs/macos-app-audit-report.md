## Stability observations

The primary interval was **14:43:28–15:33:48 EDT (50 min 19 s)** on one short-program SDR tune, PID 76642. Seven natural rollovers were recorded during that interval; the same tune continued through the later UI checks and another rollover until the sleep deadline stopped playback. At 15:38:06 the player was still advancing; the next capture at approximately 15:38:50 showed **Playback stopped by timer**. The 30-minute preset had been selected at approximately 15:08:33. No observed stall or native playback failure occurred during the primary interval. No accessibility-tree errors appeared in that interval's log segment; they resumed after returning from DVR Settings during the subsequent control check.

Between **15:08:25 and 15:09:49 EDT**, the window was occluded and AX stopped refreshing. A brief Guide/window/fullscreen round trip restored it; position advanced consistently with elapsed time, without retuning or pausing. Thus this was continuous same-tune playback, with a disclosed visibility interruption, rather than uninterrupted foreground viewing. The final on-screen pause check did not establish an actual pause.

## Questions for the user

1. **Q-01:** Should the documented macOS build recipe supply version/build Dart defines, or should Diagnostics use the bundle identity when those defines are absent?
2. **Q-02:** Should repeated Page Up/Down accumulate every channel step while loading, or is coalescing permitted?
3. **Q-03:** During an offline failure superseded by a newer tune, should the error slate identify the failing stream, the newly requested channel, or explicitly show both states?

The plan's separate lineup/station/style/title wording could not be mapped to separate controls on this build; that coverage remains open rather than inventing a product requirement. The user guide's macOS unsupported/release-status copy also remains a documentation follow-up for the port's eventual release, not a new playback defect.
