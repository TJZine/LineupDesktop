## 0.1.0 — unreleased baseline

### Across platforms

- Link Plex with a QR code or PIN, choose a Plex Home profile, and select a server and libraries.
- Generate scheduled channels from libraries, playlists, collections, and categories such as genre or decade.
- Browse the Guide with picture-in-picture playback, filter channels, and tune in to programs that are on now.
- Watch live channels with a mini Guide, direct channel entry, a sleep timer, and optional pause and seek controls.
- Choose audio and subtitle tracks and customize how text subtitles look.
- Use Channel Studio to create, edit, or duplicate custom channels and order hand-picked programs.
- Adjust themes, Guide hours, text size, motion, and focus indicators in Settings.
- Enable redacted Diagnostics to review playback and connection details and copy a support report.

### Windows

- Run the portable app on Windows 10 or 11 x64 with native playback and fullscreen HDR matching on compatible displays.

### macOS

- Use the Apple Silicon development build for native playback, picture-in-picture Guide browsing, and macOS fullscreen controls.

### Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV

- Sideload the APK on Android TV and Google TV devices running Android 9 or later, or on compatible Fire TVs running Fire OS. Browse the Guide and play channels with a D-pad remote.

### Known limitations

- Windows builds are unsigned, and Smart App Control blocks them when enabled.
- macOS is available for private testing while distribution and runtime validation continue.
- The Android APK requires an ARM device. Playback uses Direct Play and depends on the device's decoders. Vega OS Fire TVs cannot install the APK.
